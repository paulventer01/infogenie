# InfoGenie — Complete Feature Guide

**InfoGenie** is an AI-powered marketing intelligence and campaign automation platform. It replaces a stack of separate tools (Brand24, Semrush, HubSpot, an ad agency, and more) with a single workspace where your team can research, create, publish, and optimise — all in one place.

---

## HOW THE PLATFORM IS ORGANISED

InfoGenie is divided into five main areas accessible from the top navigation bar:

| Tab | What it covers |
|-----|----------------|
| **Analyse** | Research competitors, monitor your brand, and understand your market |
| **Create** | Write copy, design visuals, build landing pages, and plan campaigns |
| **Reach** | Build audiences, launch ads, publish content, and handle leads |
| **Grow** | Track ad performance, measure ROI, and optimise over time |
| **Manage** | Calendars, projects, AI tools, team operations, and reporting |

## ANALYSE

### 1 · Start Here

**Dashboard**
Your mission-control view. Shows a live summary of competitor activity, brand health, campaign performance, and AI-generated alerts — all in one glance. Start here every morning to know what needs attention.

**Get Started Roadmap**
A guided 9-step checklist that walks new users through setting up InfoGenie correctly — from entering your brand and competitors through to launching your first campaign. Prevents wasted time and ensures the AI has the data it needs.

---

### 2 · Know Your Competition

**Competitor Profiles**
Deep-dive profiles for each competitor: their traffic estimates, ad spend, top content, social following, tech stack, and hiring activity. Gives you a factual picture of who you are up against rather than guesswork.

**Battle Cards**
One-page AI-generated cheat sheets for each competitor. Designed for sales and marketing teams to use in live conversations — lists their weaknesses, your advantages, and the best talking points to win deals against them.

**Battle Plan**
A strategic playbook generated by AI based on your competitor data. Sets out where to attack, which channels to prioritise, and what messaging to use. Turns raw intelligence into an actionable 90-day competitive strategy.

**Intelligence Hub**
A unified feed that aggregates all competitor signals — new ads, price changes, product updates, news mentions, and social activity — into one place. Eliminates the need to manually check multiple sources each day.

**AI Competitor War Room**
An interactive AI workspace where you can ask questions about your competitive landscape in plain English and get detailed, data-backed answers. Think of it as a research analyst available 24/7.

**Benchmark Intelligence**
Compares your key metrics (traffic, engagement, ad spend, share of voice) against the average across your industry peer group. Tells you whether you are ahead or behind — and by how much.

### 3 · Spy on What They're Doing

**Ad Library Spy**
Browse and search the live ads your competitors are currently running on Meta, Google, and TikTok. See creative, copy, format, and targeting angles. Essential for understanding what is working in your market right now.

**Organic Social Monitor**
Tracks your competitors' organic posts across Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and TikTok. Shows posting frequency, engagement rates, and top-performing content so you can benchmark your own organic strategy.

**LinkedIn Ad Spy**
Specifically monitors competitor LinkedIn Ads — useful for B2B brands where LinkedIn is a primary channel. Shows sponsored post creative and copy that is actively running.

**Ad Swipe File**
A personal library where you can save ads you find interesting or effective. Organise inspiration for your own campaigns and share with your creative team.

**Tech Stack Detector**
Identifies the marketing and analytics tools a competitor's website is running — CRM, email platform, analytics, chat widgets, and more. Useful for understanding their infrastructure and finding switching opportunities.

**Pricing Watcher**
Monitors competitor pricing pages for changes. Sends an alert the moment a rival adjusts their prices, upgrades a tier, or changes a promotion — so you can respond quickly.

**Hiring Spy (Job Boards)**
Watches competitor job postings across major boards. New engineering hires signal product investment; new sales hires signal geographic expansion. Understand their strategy from their hiring patterns.

**Maps Intel (Local Competitors)**
For businesses with a local presence, tracks competitors on Google Maps — their reviews, ratings, photos, and updates. Helps local businesses stay ahead of nearby rivals.

**Change Monitor (Page Alerts)**
Monitors competitor web pages (pricing, homepage, product pages) and alerts you when content changes. Never miss a rebrand, new feature launch, or pricing update.

**AI Web Extractor**
Uses AI to extract structured data from any web page — product details, pricing tables, contact information, reviews — without needing to write code. Saves hours of manual research.

**Scraping Recipe Library**
A library of pre-built data extraction templates for common sources (e-commerce sites, directories, review platforms). Run them on demand to pull fresh competitor data.

**Dataset Marketplace**
Access to curated third-party datasets — industry benchmarks, contact databases, demographic data — that can enrich your competitive research and audience targeting.

**Resilient Tracker**
Monitors competitor pages that frequently change structure or block scrapers. Uses adaptive techniques to keep your tracking running even when competitors update their sites.

### 4 · Read the Market

**Share of Voice**
Measures what percentage of total online conversation in your category your brand owns versus competitors. A rising share of voice is an early indicator of growing market presence before revenue follows.

**Trending Topics**
Shows which topics, keywords, and conversations are gaining momentum in your industry right now. Use this to create timely content and get ahead of trends before they peak.

**Crisis Radar**
Monitors the web for sudden negative spikes in mentions, sentiment drops, or viral complaints about your brand. Gives you early warning of a potential PR crisis so you can respond before it escalates.

### 5 · Understand Your Customers

**ICP Studio (Your Buyer)**
AI-generated Ideal Customer Profiles built from your competitor data, market research, and your own customer inputs. Defines who you are really selling to — their job titles, pain points, goals, objections, and where they spend time online.

**Voice of Customer**
Aggregates and analyses what customers are saying about your brand and competitors in reviews, forums, and social media. Surfaces the exact language your customers use — invaluable for writing copy that resonates.

**Review Aggregator**
Pulls reviews of your brand (and competitors) from Google, Trustpilot, G2, Capterra, and App Store into one place. Identifies recurring themes — both praise and complaints — that should inform your product and messaging.

**Glassdoor Sentiment**
Analyses employee reviews on Glassdoor for you and competitors. Internal culture problems often predict external product or service problems. Useful for due diligence and understanding competitor talent dynamics.

### 6 · Listen for Mentions

**Web & News Mentions**
Monitors thousands of news sites, blogs, and online publications for any mention of your brand, competitors, or keywords. Essential for staying on top of press coverage and third-party commentary.

**Reddit Research**
Searches Reddit for threads and comments about your brand, category, or competitors. Reddit is one of the most authentic sources of unfiltered customer opinion — users say what they really think.

**Reddit Live Pulse**
A real-time feed of Reddit activity around your tracked keywords. Useful for spotting conversations as they happen so you can engage at the right moment.

**X / Twitter Pulse**
A live feed of Twitter/X mentions and conversations. Tracks hashtags, keywords, and brand mentions in real time. Social media crises often start on Twitter — this tool catches them early.

**YouTube Monitor**
Tracks YouTube videos mentioning your brand or competitors — including review videos, tutorials, and comparisons. Video content has high purchase influence; knowing what is being said matters.

**Comment Miner (Content Ideas)**
Analyses the comments on YouTube videos and social posts in your niche to find the questions, frustrations, and desires your audience is expressing. Turns community conversations into a content idea machine.

**Podcast Monitor**
Searches podcast transcripts for mentions of your brand, competitors, or key topics. Podcasts are growing in influence — this tool ensures you know when you or your market is being discussed.

**Quora Q&A Mining**
Finds the questions people are asking on Quora about your industry, product category, or competitors. Great for content strategy, FAQ creation, and understanding the information gaps in your market.

**Newsletter Tracker**
Monitors competitor newsletters and industry publications, extracting the topics and angles they are covering. Helps you stay informed about what content is being distributed in your niche.

### 7 · Brand Intelligence

**Reputation Score**
A single 0–100 score that combines your sentiment, mention volume, share of voice, and crisis history into one brand health number. Gives you a single metric to track over time and report to leadership.

**Presence Score**
Measures how prominent your brand is across search, social media, news, communities, and influencer channels — with a score out of 100 for each dimension. Identifies which channels need more investment.

**Media Value (AVE)**
Calculates the Advertising Value Equivalent of your earned media — what you would have paid for the same exposure in paid advertising. Helps justify PR and content spend by putting a dollar value on coverage.

**AI Anomaly Detector**
Automatically detects when your brand's mention volume or sentiment spikes or drops abnormally. AI explains what likely caused the change and recommends what action to take. Catches issues you might otherwise miss.

**Intent Radar**
Scans forums, Reddit, and social media to find people showing purchase intent for your product category — from early research through to ready-to-buy signals. Feeds warm prospects directly into your outreach pipeline.

**Hashtag Tracker**
Tracks specific hashtags across Twitter/X, Instagram, and TikTok — showing posting volume, velocity, top influencers using the tag, and sentiment. Essential for campaign hashtag monitoring and trend tracking.

**Influence Score**
Ranks every source and author mentioning your brand by their real influence — factoring in follower count, engagement rate, domain authority, and reach. Helps you focus relationship-building on the voices that actually move opinion.

**Project Comparison**
Compares your brand side-by-side against up to four competitors across traffic, social following, sentiment, mentions, share of voice, and domain authority. Gives a clear visual answer to "how do we stack up?"

**Geolocation Insights**
Shows where in the world your brand is being discussed — broken down by region, with local sentiment, key topics, and strategic notes for each geography. Essential for brands expanding into new markets.

**UGC Discovery**
Finds user-generated content about your brand — customer reviews, organic social posts, unboxing videos, and testimonials. Curate and approve the best pieces to repurpose across your marketing channels.

### 8 · Find Search Opportunity

**Keyword Explorer**
Researches keywords in your industry — showing search volume, difficulty, cost-per-click, and trend direction. The foundation of any SEO or paid search strategy.

**Question Mining (What People Ask)**
Finds the questions your target audience is typing into search engines. Directly informs your FAQ pages, blog content, and chatbot scripts with language that matches real search intent.

**Social Listening**
Monitors conversations across social platforms for your tracked keywords and topics. Broader than brand mentions — tracks the whole conversation happening around your category.

**Media Intelligence**
Aggregates coverage from news outlets, trade press, and blogs into a single feed filtered by your industry keywords. Stay informed about what journalists and publishers are covering in your space.

**Keyword Map**
Maps your target keywords to the specific pages on your website best suited to rank for them. Prevents keyword cannibalisation and ensures your SEO efforts are efficiently organised.

**Search Intent Map**
Categorises keywords by search intent (informational, navigational, commercial, transactional) and maps them to the appropriate stage of your funnel. Ensures you create the right type of content for each keyword.

**Content Gaps vs Rivals**
Identifies topics that your competitors rank for but you do not. These gaps represent direct SEO opportunities where creating new content could capture traffic you are currently missing.

**Live Google SERP**
Shows the live search results page for any keyword — including ads, featured snippets, People Also Ask boxes, and organic rankings. Understand exactly what a user sees before clicking through.

**SERP Rank Tracker**
Tracks your website's Google ranking positions for your target keywords over time. Essential for measuring whether your SEO efforts are working.

### 8 · Backlinks

**Backlink Explorer**
Shows all the websites linking to you or a competitor. Backlinks are a primary Google ranking factor — understanding who links to rivals reveals link-building opportunities for your own site.

**Backlink Health Monitor**
Monitors your existing backlink profile for lost, broken, or toxic links. Alerts you when valuable links disappear so you can reclaim them, and flags harmful links that could damage your rankings.

**Link Prospector**
Identifies websites likely to link to your content based on what they have linked to before. Builds a prioritised outreach list for your link-building campaigns.

**Templates Library**

### 2 · Write the Message

Generates full-length blog posts, articles, white papers, and guides using AI. Informed by your brand voice, target keywords, and competitive research so the output is relevant and optimised from the start.

Tests multiple headline options against each other using AI scoring and predicted click-through rates. A strong headline is the biggest single lever on content performance — this tool takes the guesswork out.

Generates professional press releases in the correct journalistic format. Ensures your news announcements have the structure and language that gets picked up by publications.

**Cold Email Writer**
Creates personalised cold outreach emails using AI. Incorporates the recipient's company, role, and likely pain points to produce emails that feel researched rather than mass-blasted.

**Email Personalizer (1-to-1)**
Takes a base email and produces hundreds of personalised variants at scale — customising the opening, body references, and call to action for each recipient. Dramatically improves reply rates.

**Email Broadcast + Tracking**
Sends mass email campaigns to your list with full open, click, and reply tracking. Includes deliverability optimisation to ensure emails reach the inbox rather than spam.

**WhatsApp Channel**
Sends marketing messages, notifications, and automated sequences via WhatsApp. Reaches audiences who prefer messaging apps over email — typically achieves much higher open rates.

**AI Voice Caller**
Makes automated outbound phone calls using a realistic AI voice. Can qualify leads, follow up on proposals, or deliver appointment reminders — all without human involvement.

**Reply Assistant (Inbound)**
Monitors your inbound email and social DMs and drafts AI-suggested replies. Keeps response times fast and tone consistent even when your team is stretched.

**Localization (40+ Languages)**
Translates and culturally adapts your marketing content into 40+ languages. Goes beyond literal translation to ensure idioms, tone, and cultural references land correctly in each market.

## REACH

### 1 · Build the Audience

**Audience Builder (Start)**
The starting point for building a targetable audience. Define your criteria — demographics, interests, behaviours, job titles — and the AI builds a profile ready to use across ad platforms.

**Lookalike Audiences**
Takes your existing customer list and finds new people with similar characteristics across ad platforms. One of the highest-ROI audience strategies available in digital advertising.

**Dynamic Audiences (Live)**
Audiences that automatically update in real time based on user behaviour — site visits, email opens, purchase history. Ensures your ads are always targeting the most relevant segment without manual refreshing.

**Customer Journey Builder**
Maps and automates the sequence of touchpoints a customer experiences — from first ad impression through to purchase and retention. Visual drag-and-drop interface for designing multi-step automated workflows.

**In-App Survey Builder**
Creates surveys that appear within your product or website to capture real-time customer feedback. First-party data collected here feeds directly into your audience segmentation.

**Visual Email Designer**
A drag-and-drop email template designer for creating beautiful, brand-consistent email campaigns without HTML knowledge. Previews across devices and email clients before sending.

**AI Segment Suggestions**
AI analyses your customer data and suggests high-value audience segments you may not have considered — based on behaviour patterns, purchase history, and engagement signals.

**Audience → Ad Platform Sync (Meta / Google)**
Pushes your custom and lookalike audiences directly into Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads so they are immediately available for campaign targeting. Eliminates manual export/import steps.

**Omnichannel Composer (Email · SMS · WhatsApp · Voice · Push)**
Creates a single campaign message and automatically formats and distributes it across every channel simultaneously — email, SMS, WhatsApp, phone call, and browser push notification.

**Smart Send Time Optimizer**
Uses AI to determine the optimal send time for each individual recipient based on their past engagement patterns. Sending at the right time can lift open rates by 20–30%.

**AI Campaign Translator**
Translates entire campaign sequences — emails, ads, landing pages — into multiple languages while preserving tone and intent. Enables efficient multi-market campaign deployment.

### 5 · Get Found in Search & AI

**AI Visibility & Search Pulse**
Tracks how often your brand appears in AI-generated answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) alongside traditional Google results. As AI search grows, this visibility increasingly drives traffic.

**GEO Audit (ChatGPT · Perplexity)**
Audits how well your content is optimised to appear in Generative Engine Optimisation — the emerging discipline of getting your brand cited in AI assistant responses.

**Local SEO (Google Maps)**
Optimises your Google Business Profile and tracks local search rankings. For businesses with a physical location, local SEO often drives more qualified foot traffic than any paid channel.

**On-Page SEO Audit**
Analyses individual web pages for SEO issues — missing meta tags, thin content, broken links, page speed, structured data, and more. Provides a prioritised fix list.

**Content Score Auto-Optimize**
Scores your existing content against the top-ranking pages for your target keywords and automatically suggests improvements to close the gap. Improves existing content faster than creating new pages.

**Full-Site SEO Crawler**
Crawls your entire website like a search engine to surface technical SEO issues — broken links, duplicate content, missing canonical tags, redirect chains — at scale.

**SEO Roadmap (9-Step Guide)**
A personalised, step-by-step SEO action plan generated based on your current site, competitors, and keyword targets. Gives you a prioritised order of what to fix and create first.

**Web Vitals (Page Speed)**
Measures your Core Web Vitals scores — the page speed and user experience metrics that Google uses as a ranking factor. Identifies which pages need performance improvements.

**Accessibility Audit**
Tests your web pages against WCAG accessibility standards. Accessibility issues can result in legal risk and affect SEO — this audit surfaces and prioritises what to fix.

**Social Tags Audit**
Checks your Open Graph and Twitter Card tags — the metadata that controls how your pages appear when shared on social media. Correct tags increase click-through rates from social shares.

**Email Deliverability Audit**
Tests your email sending infrastructure — SPF, DKIM, DMARC records, and blacklist status. Poor deliverability can cause even excellent emails to land in spam rather than the inbox.

**SEO Task Manager**
A project management tool specifically for SEO work — assign tasks, track progress, and log completed fixes. Keeps complex, multi-month SEO programmes organised and accountable.

**Embeddable Lead-Gen Widget**
Creates a small embeddable widget you can add to your website or partners' sites to capture leads. Useful for growing your list from third-party traffic.

## GROW

### 1 · Set the Targets

**Goals & Targets**
Sets your marketing KPIs — revenue targets, lead volume, ROAS goals, and growth rates — in a central place. Everything in the platform references these targets to contextualise performance.

**KPI Tracker (Live)**
A live dashboard tracking your actual performance against every KPI you have set. Updates automatically as new data arrives. Removes the need for manual reporting spreadsheets.

**Action Center (Do This Next)**
AI analyses your current performance across all channels and tells you the single most impactful action you should take right now. Cuts through the noise of too much data to give a clear priority.

---

### 2 · See Ad Performance

**Meta Ads Insights**
A detailed analytics view of your Facebook and Instagram ad performance — broken down by campaign, ad set, creative, and audience. Identify what is working and what is wasting budget.

**Google Ads Insights**
The equivalent performance view for your Google Search, Display, Shopping, and YouTube campaigns. Shows keyword-level performance alongside campaign and creative data.

**TikTok Ads Insights**
Tracks performance for your TikTok paid campaigns — view rates, click-through, conversions, and cost efficiency. TikTok's native analytics are limited; this view gives you the full picture.

**Organic Social Analytics**
Measures the reach, engagement, and follower growth of your organic social posts across all platforms. Helps you understand which content is building your audience without paid support.

**Post Performance**
A granular view of how every individual post you have published is performing. Ranks posts by engagement, reach, and conversion to inform future content decisions.

**AI Campaign Optimizer (Auto)**
Automatically monitors your ad campaigns and makes real-time adjustments — pausing underperforming ads, reallocating budget to winners, and refreshing creative before fatigue sets in. Runs 24/7 without manual intervention.

**Autonomous Marketing Operator**
An AI agent that manages entire marketing workflows end-to-end — from identifying an opportunity, creating the content, launching the campaign, and reporting the result — with minimal human input required.

**Safe Agent (Propose → Approve → Execute)**
The cautious version of the Autonomous Operator. The AI proposes an action, a human approves it, and then the AI executes. Gives you AI-powered efficiency while keeping human oversight on every decision.

---

### 3 · Track Full-Funnel ROI

**GSC & GA4 Hub**
Connects your Google Search Console and Google Analytics 4 data into a single view alongside your paid media performance. Gives you the full picture from organic search through to conversion.

**Amplitude AI Agents**
Connects to your Amplitude product analytics data and uses AI agents to surface insights, anomalies, and growth opportunities from your user behaviour data.

**Blended Performance (CAC)**
Calculates your true blended Customer Acquisition Cost across every channel — paid, organic, referral, and direct. The single most important unit economics metric for any growth business.

**Attribution Modeling**
Models which marketing touchpoints actually deserve credit for driving conversions. Moves beyond last-click attribution to give a more accurate picture of what is really working.

**True ROAS (Margin-Aware)**
Calculates your Return on Ad Spend using actual gross margin rather than revenue. A campaign might look profitable on a revenue basis but lose money at margin — this tool reveals the truth.

**iROAS Incrementality**
Measures the incremental uplift your advertising actually caused — the additional revenue that would not have happened without the ad spend. More accurate than standard ROAS, which includes customers who would have bought anyway.

**Conversion Recovery (CAPI)**
Implements Conversions API (CAPI) for Meta and Google to recover conversion signals that are lost due to iOS privacy changes and ad blockers. Recovers 15–40% of conversions that would otherwise go unmeasured.

**Churn-Risk Scorer**
Identifies customers showing behavioural signals of being likely to churn — reduced usage, declining engagement, late payments. Enables proactive retention outreach before the customer cancels.
