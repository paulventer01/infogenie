# InfoGenie — Complete Feature Guide

> AI-powered marketing intelligence and campaign automation platform.  
> Everything a marketing team needs to research, create, reach, grow, and manage — in one place.

---

## How InfoGenie is organised

InfoGenie follows a logical marketing workflow across five areas:

| Area | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Analyse** | Research your market, competitors, customers, and keywords |
| **Create** | Write copy, design visuals, build pages, plan campaigns |
| **Reach** | Build audiences, find leads, launch ads, publish content |
| **Grow** | Track performance, optimise ads, improve SEO, convert more |
| **Manage** | Schedule, report, run the team, and configure the platform |
