Features I Would Add To Make It Truly Unique
1. AI Competitor War Room

Current:

Competitor profiles
Ad monitoring
Pricing monitoring

New:

An AI system that automatically predicts competitor moves.

Example:

Competitor X has hired a Head of Partnerships, increased Google spend 32%, updated pricing, and launched 4 new landing pages.

Confidence 82%:

They are preparing to enter the South African market within 90 days.

This becomes a strategic intelligence platform rather than a marketing platform.

Potential value increase:
Very high.

2. AI Revenue Forecast Engine

Currently you track performance.

Add:

"Tell me what revenue will look like if I increase Google budget by R50,000."

The AI models:

Leads
Conversion rates
CAC
LTV
Revenue

Outputs:

Best case
Expected case
Worst case

This would be extremely valuable to CEOs.

3. Digital Twin of Your Business

This is where the market is going.

Create:

An AI clone of the company.

Ask:

What happens if I increase ad spend?
What happens if I launch in the UK?
What happens if I cut Meta spend?

The AI simulates outcomes.

Almost nobody currently offers this effectively.

Finds 1,000 prospects
Scores them

No human required.
