What would actually make it unique
Honestly, you're past the point where adding a 201st feature differentiates you. Almost every module here competes with a best-in-class incumbent (Semrush, Ahrefs, Crayon, Jasper, AdCreative, HubSpot, Brandwatch), and "we have all of it in one place" is a positioning, not a moat — bundles get unbundled. The features that would make InfoGenie genuinely hard to copy are the ones that compound into a data or trust advantage. In rough priority:
A cross-customer benchmark data moat. Right now most of your data is rented through third-party APIs (Apollo, Amplitude, Clarity, scraping). The asset competitors can't replicate is your own network: anonymized, aggregated benchmarks across all InfoGenie accounts — "your CPA vs. the InfoGenie median for SaaS in your region," "this creative angle is outperforming category baseline by 30%." That turns usage into a proprietary dataset and a network effect. This is the single highest-leverage thing to build.
Media-mix modeling and budget simulation. You measure outcomes well (attribution, True ROAS, iROAS) but I don't see forward-looking allocation. A "marketing digital twin" that simulates expected ROAS/CAC before launch and recommends the optimal budget split across channels — MMM plus scenario planning — is both hot post-iOS14 and a natural extension of data you already hold.
A compliance / brand-safety guardrail layer. Almost nobody does this well, and it's a wedge into high-value regulated verticals (finance, health, crypto — and notably the forex world we were just in). An engine that checks ad copy and landing pages against FTC/GDPR/ESMA/FCA-type rules, flags risky claims, and manages required disclaimers would let you own verticals horizontal tools avoid.
Trustworthy autonomous execution, not just autopilot. You have a lot of "autopilot," but the frontier that builds trust is the agent that proposes → simulates → requests approval → executes → measures → rolls back, with hard budget guardrails and a full audit log. Autonomy plus provable safety, together, is rare.
Data provenance as a first-class feature. I noticed "data honesty controls" and "demo-data badges" in the admin — lean into that hard. In an era of AI-hallucination skepticism, every insight carrying a source link, freshness timestamp, and a real-vs-AI-estimated indicator could be a brand-defining differentiator ("the marketing platform you can audit"). Buyers in 2026 are explicitly discounting "AI marketing" versus provable AI value, so this doubles as a valuation lever.
Verticalized playbooks over horizontal sprawl. Pre-built, benchmarked packs per industry (e-commerce, SaaS, local, finance) with the right channels, templates, and compliance baked in. This makes onboarding fast and gives sales a sharp story instead of "we do everything."
The meta-point: pick one or two of these to be genuinely best-in-world at, anchored by the benchmark data moat. Breadth is your reach; depth plus proprietary data is what makes it valuable.
