1 · Vision & Design Philosophy
What is InfoGenie?
The single platform that replaces your entire marketing toolstack — from competitive intelligence to campaign automation, content creation to compliance.

The Core Problem InfoGenie Solves

The average marketing team today pays for 12–18 separate SaaS tools — Semrush for SEO, Brandwatch for monitoring, HubSpot for CRM, Meta Business Suite for ads, Canva for creative, Mailchimp for email, and so on. Each tool has its own login, data silo, billing, and learning curve. Teams spend more time context-switching between tools than actually making marketing decisions.

InfoGenie is designed as the operating system for modern marketing teams. It consolidates over 115 specialised marketing intelligence and automation tools into a single, AI-native platform — covering the complete marketing loop: research → plan → create → publish → optimise → report.

Design Philosophy

  • AI-first, not AI-added: Every feature is built around AI reasoning — GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Cloudflare Llama are embedded in every workflow, not bolted on as an afterthought.
  • Actionable over analytical: InfoGenie doesn't just surface data — it tells you what to do with it. Every report ends with a prioritised action list.
  • Competitive intelligence as a moat: The platform is built around understanding your competitors as deeply as your own business — ad creative, pricing changes, hiring patterns, backlink profiles, keyword rankings.
  • Full-funnel coverage: From top-of-funnel SEO and brand monitoring all the way through to lead scoring, campaign launch, conversion optimisation, and churn prediction.
  • Automation with human guardrails: The Safe Agent and Approval Workflows system ensures AI can propose and simulate changes, but humans approve before execution — preventing costly mistakes.
  • Multi-tenant & agency-ready: Built with multi-tenancy from the ground up — agencies can manage multiple client workspaces from one login.

Multi-tenant Architecture

Every piece of data in InfoGenie is isolated by tenant. This means agencies can manage dozens of client workspaces, each with their own competitor data, campaigns, leads, and settings — all from one login. The permission matrix system allows granular feature access control per user role within a tenant.

AI Safety Architecture

InfoGenie's AI pipeline uses a strict validation pattern: every LLM response is validated as strict JSON → checked against a /^_DUMMY/i key gate to detect non-responses → falls back to a template response if validation fails → then persists to PostgreSQL. This means AI features never silently fail — users always get a meaningful response.

Pricing Strategy Principles

  • Value-based pricing: Price against the cost of the tools being replaced ($500–$2,000/month), not cost-of-goods. InfoGenie at $497 replacing a $1,200 toolstack is an easy sell.
