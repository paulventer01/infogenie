## What we process and why
As a Processor, we process personal data on our customers' instructions to
provide the InfoGenie platform:

| Data | Purpose | Basis (set by the customer as Controller) |
|---|---|---|
| Identifiers (name, email, phone, device/platform ids) | Identity resolution, messaging, personalisation | Consent or legitimate interest, per the Controller |
| Engagement & behavioural events | Segmentation, prediction, measurement | Consent or legitimate interest |
| Consent & preference state | Honour reachability at send time | Legal obligation / consent |

We do **not** use customer personal data to train cross-tenant AI models without
explicit, revocable consent.

## Consent & your choices
Consent is recorded per person, per channel, per purpose, with provenance.
Withdrawal is honoured immediately and across every channel and campaign.
Suppression (unsubscribe / do-not-contact) is enforced at send time.

## Your rights
Access, portability, correction, deletion, objection, and restriction, exercisable
within statutory windows across every store — including vector embeddings, caches,
logs and backups. Requests are actioned through platform DSAR tooling and recorded
in the audit rail. Contact the relevant customer (Controller) or `[dpo@…]`.

## AI transparency
Where you interact with an AI system or receive AI-generated content, this is
disclosed in line with applicable law (e.g. the EU AI Act) and our published
guidelines.

## Retention
Personal data is retained per a published schedule per data class and deleted
automatically on schedule, or sooner on a valid erasure request.

## International transfers
Where data crosses borders, we use documented transfer mechanisms and offer
data-residency options where required.

## Sub-processors
We use the sub-processors listed in our Sub-Processor Register (including model
providers), each bound by equivalent data-protection obligations.
