## Compete

Everything you need to track, analyse, and outmaneuver your competitors.

---

### Battle Cards
**What it does:** Instantly generates a structured competitive battle card for any competitor — 4 strengths, 4 weaknesses, 3 strategic moves, and 4 counter-plays, all powered by AI.

**Benefit:** Your sales and marketing teams always know exactly what to say when a prospect mentions a competitor. Reduces lost deals caused by poor competitive positioning.

---

### Share of Voice
**What it does:** Tracks your brand's visibility relative to competitors across the web over time, presented as a stacked-area chart updated every 6 hours automatically.

**Benefit:** You see at a glance whether you're gaining or losing ground in your market — before your quarterly review, not after.

---

### Ad Library Spy
**What it does:** Searches Meta and TikTok ad libraries to surface your competitors' live ads — creative, copy, targeting signals, and how long they've been running.

**Benefit:** Stop guessing what's working for competitors. Swipe winning ad formats and messaging angles for your own campaigns.

---

### Pricing Watcher
**What it does:** Monitors competitor pricing pages on a schedule, scrapes changes automatically, and alerts you when prices shift.

**Benefit:** Never get caught off-guard by a competitor price drop. React to pricing changes the same day they happen.

---

### Tech Stack Detector
**What it does:** Analyses any website (up to 5 at once) and reveals the full technology stack — CRM, analytics, ecommerce platform, marketing tools, hosting, and more.

**Benefit:** Understand how competitors are built, identify tools you should be using, and find gaps in their stack you can exploit in sales conversations.

---

### Web Vitals Auditor
**What it does:** Runs a full Google PageSpeed audit on any URL — both mobile and desktop — surfacing load times, Core Web Vitals scores, and the top 6 actionable fixes.

**Benefit:** A faster website converts better and ranks higher. Know exactly which performance issues to fix first for the biggest ROI.

---

### SERP Position Tracker
**What it does:** Tracks your exact Google search ranking for any keyword across 15 countries, with a full history of position changes over time.

**Benefit:** Know whether your SEO efforts are actually moving the needle, and catch ranking drops before they hurt your traffic.

---

### SEO On-Page Auditor
**What it does:** Audits any webpage against 17 on-page SEO checks (title length, meta description, H1, canonical, structured data, image alt text, internal links, and more) and gives an A–F grade with prioritised fixes.

**Benefit:** Find and fix the SEO issues holding your pages back from ranking, ranked in order of impact so you fix the most important things first.

---

### SEO Task Manager
**What it does:** Turns SEO audit results into a live task board. Import findings from any audit as prioritised tasks (P1/P2/P3), assign them, set due dates, and mark them done. Re-auditing automatically closes tasks that are now fixed.

**Benefit:** Audit findings no longer get lost in a spreadsheet. Turns a one-time snapshot into an ongoing improvement workflow.

---

### Embeddable Audit Widget
**What it does:** Generates a paste-once JavaScript widget you can put on your own website. Visitors enter their URL, get an instant SEO score, and you capture their email as a lead.

**Benefit:** A fully automated lead-generation machine for agencies and consultants — visitors self-qualify by caring enough about their SEO to check it.

---

### Schema.org / JSON-LD Generator
**What it does:** Builds valid structured data markup (JSON-LD) for 8 schema types — Organization, Article, Product, FAQ, Local Business, Breadcrumbs, Event, and Recipe — through a simple form, no coding required.

**Benefit:** Structured data helps Google, ChatGPT, and Perplexity understand and cite your content correctly, improving both search rankings and AI visibility.

---

### Unified Conversation Inbox
**What it does:** Pulls mentions and conversations from 7 sources (Reddit, Twitter/X, Reviews, Quora, Glassdoor, Newsletters, Chatbot) into one unified inbox with status tracking, sentiment labels, notes, and assignees.

**Benefit:** Stop switching between 7 tabs to manage brand conversations. Everything is in one place with a clear workflow from new → replied → resolved.

---

### Conversion Boosters
**What it does:** Two paste-once website widgets: (1) a rotating social-proof popup ("Sarah from Cape Town just signed up") and (2) an exit-intent popup that captures emails before visitors leave.

**Benefit:** Lift conversion rates on existing traffic without touching your ad budget. Social proof and exit capture are among the highest-ROI CRO tactics available.

---

### Review Aggregator
**What it does:** Pulls reviews from Trustpilot, G2, Google, Capterra, and TripAdvisor for any brand. Supports side-by-side comparison of 2–4 competitors.

**Benefit:** Instantly know what customers love and hate about your competitors — and use that to sharpen your own positioning and product roadmap.

---

### Glassdoor Sentiment
**What it does:** Analyses employee reviews on Glassdoor for any company — overall rating, CEO approval, culture signals, top complaints, and strategic insights about company direction.

**Benefit:** Understand a competitor's internal health. High turnover and culture problems often precede product slowdowns and customer churn — good signals for sales timing.

---

### Job Board Spy
**What it does:** Scrapes open job listings for any company from LinkedIn, Indeed, and careers pages, breaks them down by department, and generates strategic signals about what the hiring pattern reveals.

**Benefit:** A company's hiring plan is a public roadmap. Discover if a competitor is expanding into your territory, building a new product team, or struggling (no new hires).

---

### TikTok Downloader
**What it does:** Downloads up to 25 TikTok videos at once (no watermark), with metadata — caption, hashtags, music, author, views, likes, and comments.

**Benefit:** Build a permanent swipe file of competitor and industry-leading creative. Never lose access to a video that gets taken down.

---

### Newsletter Tracker
**What it does:** Monitors any competitor's email newsletter (Substack, Beehiiv, Mailchimp) and extracts the subject lines, send dates, and preview content from the last 10 issues.

**Benefit:** Know what messaging and angles your competitors are testing with their email audience — before they roll it out to their full ad spend.

---

### Change Monitor
**What it does:** Watches any competitor URL for content changes. When a significant change is detected (≥5% word delta), AI classifies the change type (pricing, features, messaging, design) and delivers a strategic insight.

**Benefit:** Get notified the moment a competitor changes their pricing page, homepage, or product description — so you can respond immediately instead of finding out months later.

---

### Resilient Tracker
**What it does:** Tracks specific data points on any competitor page (e.g. "current price of Plan A") using a two-source fallback (Firecrawl + Perplexity) so tracking continues even when a page blocks scrapers.

**Benefit:** Reliable, persistent competitor data tracking that doesn't break every time a site changes its structure or adds bot protection.

---

### AI Competitor War Room
**What it does:** Gathers live signals about a competitor (recent hires, ad spend changes, new pages, pricing shifts, PR, geographic expansion) and uses AI to predict their next strategic move with a confidence score and recommended counter-move.

**Benefit:** Stop reacting to competitors — start anticipating them. The War Room tells you what move is coming so you can prepare your counter before they execute.

---

### Business Acquisition Scanner
**What it does:** Scans for M&A opportunities — struggling competitors, businesses for sale, fast-growing sectors, or franchise opportunities — scored on strategic value, price attractiveness, and growth trajectory.

**Benefit:** Identify acquisition targets at the right moment. Distress signals and market gaps surface opportunities that aren't on traditional deal platforms yet.

---

### AI Answer SOV (Share of Voice)
**What it does:** Tracks whether AI engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity) mention your brand when answering buyer questions, and which competitors get cited instead. Runs sweeps across multiple providers and shows brand citation percentage over time.

**Benefit:** AI-generated answers are becoming the first touchpoint for buyers. This tool tells you whether you exist in AI's worldview — and what to do if you don't.

---

### Proprietary Data Products
**What it does:** Three crowd-sourced intelligence datasets: (1) Creative Performance Index benchmarking ad formats by CTR/CVR/ROAS, (2) Offer Benchmark Network comparing pricing model conversion rates, and (3) Emerging Market Signals generated by AI from live data.

**Benefit:** Access competitive intelligence that no single company could generate alone — industry-wide performance benchmarks built from aggregated platform data.

---

### LLM Knowledge Base
**What it does:** Auto-generates structured knowledge bases (FAQs, how-tos, product descriptions, comparisons) optimised for citation by AI engines. Exports llms.txt files, JSON-LD schema, and step-by-step submission instructions.

**Benefit:** The brands that feed AI engines the right information get cited in AI answers. This tool handles the entire process — from writing optimised content to telling you exactly how to submit it.

## Grow

Launch campaigns, optimise spend, and model revenue growth.

---

### Campaign Launch
**What it does:** Creates and launches ad campaigns across Google, Meta, and TikTok from a single interface. Campaigns auto-register with the AI Optimizer the moment they're created.

**Benefit:** Cut the time from "approved brief" to "live campaign" from hours to minutes, across all platforms simultaneously.

---

### AI Optimizer
**What it does:** An always-on ad optimisation engine running on a four-cycle schedule: hourly insight ingest, 6-hour pause/scale rules, 12-hour budget reallocation using multi-armed bandit testing, and 24-hour creative refresh.

**Benefit:** Your ad spend is continuously being optimised — not just when you remember to check it. Catches underperforming campaigns and reallocates budget to winners automatically.

---

### Landing Page Builder
**What it does:** Generates a complete, responsive landing page in seconds — hero, features, how-it-works steps, testimonials, FAQs, and CTA — all based on your brief. Live iframe preview included.

**Benefit:** Go from brief to publish-ready landing page in under 2 minutes, without a designer or developer. Every page is built to convert.

---

### CRO Lab *(Conversion Rate Optimisation)*
**What it does:** A structured experimentation environment for improving conversion rates on landing pages, emails, and ads — with hypothesis tracking, test setup, and results analysis.

**Benefit:** Turn "let's try this" into a structured process that compounds over time. Each test that lands generates permanent, measurable revenue lift.

---

### Link-in-Bio
**What it does:** Generates a mobile-optimised link-in-bio page with your key links, brand colours, and social handles — ready to publish in minutes.

**Benefit:** A polished, branded single destination for all your social traffic — without needing a separate tool or developer.

---

### Booking Pages
**What it does:** Creates a simple online booking page for consultations, demos, or appointments.

**Benefit:** Capture high-intent visitors who want to talk to you, without the friction of a back-and-forth email thread.

---

### AI Revenue Forecast Engine
**What it does:** Models 90-day revenue scenarios (best/expected/worst case) based on your current spend, leads, conversion rate, AOV, CAC, and LTV. Outputs payback period, LTV/CAC ratio, and a plain-English recommendation.

**Benefit:** Make budget decisions with confidence. Know before you spend whether a budget increase will pay back in 30, 60, or 90 days.

---

### Digital Twin
**What it does:** Simulates business decisions before you make them — "what if I increase spend by 30%?", "what if I launch in the UK?", "what if I cut Meta?" — with a 90-day timeline, upsides, risks, and a recommended action.

**Benefit:** Test your biggest strategic decisions in minutes with zero financial risk. The AI tells you what's likely to happen before you commit.

---

### AI Acquisition Engine
**What it does:** A fully autonomous lead-to-meeting pipeline. Finds prospects via AI web research, scores them, generates a personalised 3-step cold email sequence, and tracks the pipeline from found → emailed → meeting booked.

**Benefit:** A self-running outbound machine that keeps generating meetings while you focus on closing. No manual prospecting required.

---

### Autonomous Marketing Operator
**What it does:** Diagnoses marketing problems and takes immediate action — rewriting ads, pausing underperformers, scaling winners, reallocating budget, proposing A/B tests — and tells you what it did and what to expect.

**Benefit:** Your marketing doesn't stop improving when you're not in the dashboard. The Operator acts on problems the moment they appear.

---

### Safe Agent
**What it does:** A human-in-the-loop autonomous execution pipeline: AI proposes an action plan, simulates the outcome (best/worst case, confidence %, risk factors), waits for your approval, then executes — with one-click rollback if needed.

**Benefit:** Get the speed of autonomous AI execution without the risk of unreviewed actions. Every step is transparent, approved, and reversible.

---

### Revenue Forecast Engine
**What it does:** AI-powered 90-day financial modelling tool that creates best, expected, and worst-case projections based on your real business inputs.

**Benefit:** Replace gut-feel budgeting with data-backed projections that account for CAC, LTV, and conversion dynamics.

---

### Media Mix Modeler
**What it does:** Recommends the optimal budget split across all your ad channels, modelling saturation curves, diminishing returns, lag effects, and cross-channel synergy for each.

**Benefit:** Stop allocating budget by gut feel or last month's ROAS. The MMM tells you exactly where the next dollar will generate the most return.

---

### Self-Healing Ad Accounts
**What it does:** Detects ad rejections across Meta, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn, and Microsoft, rewrites the copy to comply with each platform's specific policies, and prepares healed copy for immediate resubmission.

**Benefit:** Ad rejections cost you delivery time and campaign momentum. Self-Healing turns a 2-day problem into a 2-minute fix.

---

### Cross-Platform Budget Arbitrage
**What it does:** Analyses real-time ROAS, CPA, and conversion rates across platforms and recommends specific dollar shifts to maximise total return — respecting configurable guardrails on how much can move.

**Benefit:** Capture intraday performance differences between platforms before the opportunity closes. Works while you sleep.

---

### CTV & Streaming Audio
**What it does:** Creates and manages ad campaigns on Connected TV (Roku, Hulu, Amazon Fire TV, YouTube TV) and streaming audio (Spotify, Pandora, iHeartRadio, Apple Podcasts). Includes AI-generated 30-second TV scripts and audio spots.

**Benefit:** Reach audiences who've cut the cord and use ad blockers — channels where your competitors likely aren't yet.

---

### Experiment Suite
**What it does:** Tracks incrementality experiments (A/B tests, geo-lift, holdout groups, creative incrementality, audience saturation) from hypothesis to result. AI analysis interprets statistical significance and recommends next actions.

**Benefit:** Build a compounding library of proven insights about what actually drives your growth — not just what correlates with it.

## Reach

Find your audience, personalise your outreach, and deliver messages at scale.

---

### B2B Lead Finder
**What it does:** Finds real business leads using AI-powered web research — name, company, role, and contact details — filtered by industry and persona. Includes a one-click push to HubSpot.

**Benefit:** Skip the manual LinkedIn scraping. Get a list of qualified prospects in minutes, already enriched and ready to outreach.

---

### Multi-Source Lead Aggregator
**What it does:** Sweeps leads simultaneously from Perplexity AI research and Apollo.io, deduplicates them, scores them by data completeness and title match, and returns one ranked, unified list.

**Benefit:** More coverage than any single source. You get leads that Apollo misses, and Apollo data that AI-only tools can't provide.

---

### Email Personalizer
**What it does:** Takes a batch of up to 25 leads, researches each company's website automatically, and rewrites your cold email template with genuine personalisation for each recipient.

**Benefit:** Personalised cold emails get 3–5x more replies than generic ones. This tool does the research work that nobody actually does manually at scale.

---

### Cold Email Writer
**What it does:** Generates a 1–5 step cold email sequence for any audience, product, or goal — with tone control and a template fallback when AI is unavailable.

**Benefit:** A proven multi-touch outreach sequence ready in 30 seconds, instead of an afternoon.

---

### HubSpot Sync
**What it does:** Pushes leads, influencers, and contact batches directly into HubSpot CRM with deduplication by email — no CSV export/import required.

**Benefit:** Leads captured in InfoGenie appear in your CRM instantly, keeping sales and marketing pipelines in sync automatically.

---

### Dynamic Audiences
**What it does:** Builds real-time, rule-based contact segments that update automatically every 15 minutes. Each audience can be bound to a Drip email sequence, a HubSpot Static List, or an AI win-back campaign — contacts are auto-enrolled when they join and auto-removed when they leave.

**Benefit:** Your lists are always accurate without manual effort. The right contacts get the right messages the moment the trigger condition is met.

---

### Journey Builder
**What it does:** A visual workflow builder for multi-step customer journeys — trigger conditions, wait steps, branching logic, and automated actions — running on a 60-second tick.

**Benefit:** Automate complex customer lifecycle sequences (welcome series, trial-to-paid nudges, win-back flows) once and let them run indefinitely.

---

### Drip Engine
**What it does:** Sends automated multi-step email sequences to enrolled contacts, with per-step scheduling and unsubscribe handling built in.

**Benefit:** Nurture leads on autopilot. A well-built drip sequence compounds in value — every new lead that enters immediately gets the same proven sequence.

---

### Omnichannel Composer
**What it does:** Creates and sends messages across every channel — email, SMS, WhatsApp, push notification, and social — from a single interface.

**Benefit:** Meet customers where they are instead of forcing them to a single channel. Omnichannel brands see significantly higher engagement and revenue per customer.

---

### Re-engagement Agent
**What it does:** Identifies churned or at-risk contacts and generates AI-personalised win-back campaigns — automatically triggered when a contact enters a churn-risk audience.

**Benefit:** Win back customers who've gone quiet before they leave permanently. Automated win-back campaigns recover revenue that would otherwise be written off.

---

### Churn Scorer
**What it does:** Scores each contact in your list 0–100 for churn risk, with risk level, contributing signals, and a specific recommended action for each person — bulk scoring up to 25 contacts at once.

**Benefit:** Focus retention efforts on the contacts most likely to leave, before they do. Proactive intervention is 5–10x cheaper than re-acquisition.

---

### Influencer CRM
**What it does:** A full CRM for influencer relationships — prospect, contact, negotiate, and track active partnerships through a status pipeline. Includes one-click AI-drafted outreach emails.

**Benefit:** Professional influencer relationship management without a dedicated tool. Every conversation and deal stage is tracked in one place.

---

### Influencer Discovery
**What it does:** Finds relevant influencers (≥5k followers) in any niche using AI-powered research, with one-click addition to the Influencer CRM.

**Benefit:** Skip hours of manual Instagram and TikTok searching. Get a shortlist of relevant creators with follower context in minutes.

---

### Social Publisher
**What it does:** Publishes and schedules content across 15 platforms (Twitter/X, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Reddit, Bluesky, Threads, Google Business, Telegram, Snapchat, WhatsApp, Discord) from one interface. Bulk-schedule an entire Content Calendar in one click.

**Benefit:** Manage your entire social presence without switching between platforms. One brief, one click, everywhere at once.

---

### Social Analytics
**What it does:** Aggregates engagement data (impressions, likes, comments, shares, clicks, follower growth) across all connected social accounts and surfaces your top-performing posts.

**Benefit:** See what's actually working across all platforms in one view, instead of piecing it together from 15 different dashboards.

---

### Email Deliverability Auditor
**What it does:** Checks any domain's email infrastructure — MX, SPF, DKIM (19 selectors), DMARC, MTA-STS, and BIMI — and gives a weighted A–F deliverability grade.

**Benefit:** Most deliverability problems are invisible until your emails land in spam. This audit surfaces every issue before it costs you campaign performance.

---

### Reply Assistant
**What it does:** Pulls your top 30 brand mentions ranked by relevance, drafts a full reply and a 240-character X/Twitter variant for each — with tone control.

**Benefit:** Stay on top of brand conversations without spending hours crafting individual replies. Respond to more mentions, faster, without sacrificing quality.

---

### Meeting Notes
**What it does:** Summarises any meeting transcript or notes into: executive summary, key points, action items, BANT scores, deal stage, sentiment, risks, objections, and next step.

**Benefit:** No more forgetting what was said or who committed to what. Every meeting becomes a structured, actionable record in seconds.

---

### Chatbot Builder
**What it does:** Generates a complete chatbot configuration — greeting, 8–15 FAQ entries, fallback message, lead-capture fields, suggested quick replies, and a copy-paste embed snippet — with accent colour and tone control.

**Benefit:** A working lead-capture chatbot for your website in 2 minutes, no developer needed.

---

### Quora Mining
**What it does:** Finds the 8–15 most-engaged Quora questions related to your brand or industry, classified by intent (informational, comparison, recommendation, complaint) with a suggested response angle for each.

**Benefit:** Answer the exact questions your target buyers are already asking, in a forum that ranks in Google and gets read by millions.

---

### Identity Spine (CDP-lite)
**What it does:** A consent-aware unified customer profile store. Import contacts, AI-scores them for lifetime value ($0–$10k estimated LTV) and purchase propensity (0–100%), assigns lifecycle stage, and recommends next-best-action for each.

**Benefit:** A lightweight Customer Data Platform that tells you who your most valuable customers are, where they are in the lifecycle, and exactly what to do next with each of them.

---

### RCS & Apple Messages
**What it does:** Creates and sends Rich Communication Services (Android RCS) and Apple Messages for Business campaigns — rich cards, CTA buttons, suggested replies, and personalisation — with AI generation for the full creative package.

**Benefit:** RCS and Apple Messages deliver near-100% read rates compared to email's 20–30%. Reach customers through the most personal screen they own, with rich interactive content.

**What it does:** A project management workspace for marketing initiatives — tasks, owners, deadlines, and status tracking built specifically for marketing workflows.

### 7-Day Playbook

**Benefit:** Eliminates the blank-page problem at the start of every week. A clear, prioritised plan keeps the team moving.

---

### Ask InfoGenie
**What it does:** A conversational AI assistant with full context about your brand, competitors, campaigns, and marketing data — answers questions, generates ideas, and explains results.

**Benefit:** Get expert marketing guidance on demand, 24/7, without scheduling a consultant call.

### AI Providers

**Benefit:** Find the best model for any specific task in your workflow — before you commit to it in production.

### Platform APIs
**What it does:** Admin-level management of all platform API keys (the ones InfoGenie pays for on behalf of all users), with live connection tests for every integration.

**Benefit:** Full visibility and control over every external service the platform relies on — no digging through environment variables.

---

### Benchmark Intelligence
**What it does:** Anonymised cross-customer benchmarking. Submit your own metrics (CPA, CAC, ROAS, CTR, CVR, LTV, and more) and compare them against network medians for your vertical, region, and company size.

**Benefit:** Know whether your numbers are genuinely good or just good-for-you. Real industry benchmarks replace the made-up statistics in blog posts.

---

### Investor Mode
**What it does:** Generates a complete investor-ready report — 8-metric dashboard, 12-month revenue forecast, highlights, challenges, the ask, and investor narrative — and publishes it to a shareable, no-login investor portal.

**Benefit:** Create a polished investor update in minutes instead of days. The portal link means investors always see your latest numbers without email threads.

### AI Marketing Marketplace
**What it does:** A shared marketplace for marketing assets (templates, campaigns, audiences, prompt packs, landing pages, email sequences, AI agents, creatives) — publish what you've built, download what others have shared.

**Benefit:** Accelerate with assets that have already been tested and proven in the real world. Your best work can generate value for your whole team (or the community) indefinitely.

### Data Provenance
**What it does:** Tracks the source, freshness, and AI-vs-real status of every piece of intelligence in the platform — with colour-coded freshness indicators and a live breakdown by source type.

**Benefit:** Know which numbers you can act on immediately and which need verification. Prevents costly decisions based on stale or AI-estimated data.

---

### Brand Safety & Compliance Engine
**What it does:** Audits any ad copy, landing page, email, or social post against six regulatory frameworks (FTC, GDPR, FCA, ESMA, HIPAA, ASA) and returns a risk score, specific flags with required fixes, and a fully compliant rewrite.

**Benefit:** Catch compliance issues before Legal does — or worse, before a regulator does. A single violation can cost more than a year of platform fees.

**Benefit:** AI autonomy without governance risk. The right people approve the right actions, with full audit trail and the ability to undo anything.

**What it does:** Pulls live signals from your optimizer, SEO, and SERP data and synthesises 8 prioritised recommendations across budget, channel, creative, audience, SEO, lifecycle, and competitive dimensions — each with expected impact, confidence, and time-to-result.

**Benefit:** Instead of staring at dashboards wondering what to do, the Decision Engine tells you exactly what to do next, ranked by impact.

---

### Multi-Agent Swarm
**What it does:** An inter-agent orchestration engine. Build configurable chains of specialised AI agents (competitor spy, media buyer, copywriter, SEO analyst, email composer, audience builder, and more) triggered by events like ad rejection, ROAS drop, or competitor change. Each agent receives prior agents' outputs as context.

**Benefit:** Automate entire cross-functional workflows that currently require multiple people. A ROAS drop can automatically trigger a competitor check, a creative rewrite, a budget reallocation, and a Slack alert — all without human intervention.

**What it does:** A visual no-code workflow builder (like Make.com or n8n, but built into InfoGenie). Build automations from triggers (schedule, webhook, events) through action nodes (email, Slack, HTTP, AI generate, RCS, A/B split). AI builder converts plain-English descriptions into complete workflow JSON.

**Benefit:** Automate any repetitive marketing workflow without code or a separate automation tool. Describe what you want in plain English and it builds it for you.

---

### Server-Side Event Manager
**What it does:** A full server-side tracking infrastructure — create event sources, get a unique ingest token URL, and receive purchase, lead, page view, and custom events from your website, mobile app, or CRM. All PII hashed before storage.

**Benefit:** Accurate conversion data that survives iOS privacy changes, ad blockers, and browser restrictions. The tracking approaches used by enterprise ecommerce brands, available to any size business.

---

### Privacy Auto-Compliance
**What it does:** Automated compliance tooling for GDPR, CCPA, CAN-SPAM, TCPA, Australian Privacy Act, and PIPEDA — contact scrubbing, DNC checking, consent validation, and a full AI-generated compliance audit with risk score.

**Benefit:** Compliance that runs automatically, not as a quarterly manual review. Catch issues before they become violations.

---

### Brand DNA & Custom AI Training
**What it does:** Upload your best-performing ads, emails, landing pages, and brand guidelines. AI extracts your brand voice, writing style, banned phrases, and example patterns — then uses that profile to generate and score all future content.

**Benefit:** Every AI output sounds like you wrote it. Brand consistency at scale, without a brand review process for every piece of content.
