1. Executive Summary

InfoGenie is an AI-powered marketing intelligence and campaign automation platform that compresses the work of an entire growth team — competitive analyst, copywriter, media buyer, SEO strategist, and CRM operator — into one operator-grade application.

Built on a multi-LLM backbone (OpenAI GPT-4o, Anthropic Claude, Perplexity, Google Gemini, Cloudflare Workers AI) with 80+ feature tiers spanning competitive monitoring, ad generation, ad optimization, SEO, content, conversation inbox, and white-label reporting, InfoGenie is engineered as a "Brandwatch-killer + HubSpot Marketing-killer + Adcreative.ai-killer" suite at a fraction of the cost.

Highlights

  • Dual-AI Attack Plan: parallel GPT-4o + Claude analyses synthesised by a third LLM produce more reliable competitive intelligence than any single-vendor tool.
  • Real Data Discipline: every analysis is sourced from live APIs (Meta Ad Library, DataForSEO, HubSpot, Firecrawl, GSC) — no synthetic placeholders.
  • Full Closed Loop: Discover insight → generate counter-message (4 AIs in parallel) → schedule across 15 social platforms via Zernio → launch ads on Meta/Google/TikTok → optimize hourly via Multi-Armed Bandit → measure True ROAS.
  • Agentic Command Bar: natural-language operation of the entire stack via GPT-4o function calling.

Ask

Seed round: USD 750,000 for 18 months of runway to reach 500 paying customers (USD 75K MRR / USD 900K ARR) and validate enterprise expansion.

3. Problem & Opportunity

The pain

  • The average growth team uses 9–14 disconnected tools (SEMrush + Brandwatch + Hootsuite + AdCreative + HubSpot + Triple Whale + …) costing USD 3K–10K / month.
  • Insights live in one tool, creative in another, distribution in a third — handoffs lose 60%+ of intent.
  • SMBs can't afford the stack; enterprises can't move fast enough through it.
  • Most "AI marketing" tools are single-LLM wrappers with hallucination risk and no closed loop to action.

The opportunity

  • Global martech spend: USD 215B (2025) growing 13% YoY.
  • AI in marketing TAM: USD 47B by 2028 (CAGR 28%).
  • SMB & agency segment underserved by enterprise tools — InfoGenie's sweet spot.

  • YouTube + LinkedIn: weekly "Competitor Teardown" videos featuring real brands.

  • 20% recurring revenue share for agency partners (lifetime).

  • Target: 1,500 paying customers + 25 Enterprise logos by Month 24.

9. Operations & Technology

Stack

  • Backend: Node.js / Express on Replit Deployments (auto-scale)
  • Database: PostgreSQL with atomic writes + mutex locking
  • AI providers: OpenAI GPT-4o, Anthropic Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Cloudflare Workers AI (Llama 3.1) — multi-vendor for redundancy & cost optimization
  • External APIs: DataForSEO, Firecrawl, Meta Marketing, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, HubSpot, Apollo, BuiltWith, Resend, Slack, Zernio (15-platform social), Google PageSpeed

Cost discipline

  • Per-IP, per-provider, per-day LLM budget caps prevent runaway spend.
  • Automatic provider fallback (GPT-4o → GPT-4o-mini → Cloudflare Llama) for cost-sensitive operations.
  • Strict-JSON prompt pattern + template fallback prevents wasted retries.

Security & compliance

  • SSRF guards on all external URL fetches.
  • API key gating in production (INFOGENIE_API_KEY).
  • HMAC-validated inbound webhooks (Resend, HubSpot).
  • Roadmap: SOC 2 Type II by Month 18; GDPR-compliant by launch.
