# InfoGenie — Complete Feature Guide
### AI-Powered Marketing Intelligence & Campaign Automation Platform

---

## What Is InfoGenie?

InfoGenie is an all-in-one AI marketing platform that replaces a dozen separate tools with one connected system. It helps marketing teams, agencies, and business owners **spy on competitors, launch and optimise ad campaigns, create content, find leads, and automate repetitive marketing work** — all powered by AI models including GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity.

---

## Why It Matters

| Problem without InfoGenie | What InfoGenie does instead |
|---|---|
| You pay for 10+ separate tools (SEMrush, AdEspresso, HubSpot, etc.) | One platform covers all of it |
| Competitor intelligence takes days of manual research | AI gathers it in seconds |
| Ad campaigns need a specialist to optimise | The AI Optimizer runs 24/7 automatically |
| Content creation is slow and inconsistent | Brand-trained AI generates on-brand copy instantly |
| Leads slip through without follow-up | Journey Builder and Drip Engine automate outreach |
| You don't know if your marketing is working vs. industry norms | Benchmark Intelligence compares you to real peers |
